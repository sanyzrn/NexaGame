2. TEAMWORK: the player should always feel part of a group. Individual skill feeds a shared goal.
3. LIGHTWEIGHT: medium graphics, small download, smooth on weak phones, instant start inside Telegram.
4. ATTRACTIVE AND REWARDING: players should want to play again and again, and the game should make future cosmetic purchases feel desirable (hero skins, arrow effects, group banners), without pay-to-win.
5. IDENTITY: a Shahnameh / ancient Persian world, one-finger "one breath, one arrow" mechanic, where every shot matters.
